About the stay

A peaceful base in Ooty

Gemini Homestay sits on HMT, Coonoor Road, Southwick, giving guests a calm residential setting with quick access to Ooty town and major sightseeing spots. It is positioned as a high-class homestay rather than a hotel, with a focus on cleanliness, comfort, dining support, and easygoing service.

The setting works well for family holidays, friend groups, newlyweds looking for a quieter stay, and corporate teams that want a practical place to gather and rest between outings.
