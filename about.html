About Gemini Homestay

A calm homestay, close to town

Gemini Homestay is located in Ooty, Tamil Nadu, around five minutes from the town center and within easy reach of major attractions. The atmosphere is quiet, practical, and welcoming, with a focus on comfort rather than hotel formality.

Guests come here for a homely base that is easy to live in: comfortable rooms, dining and kitchen support, clean spaces, and attentive service. It suits families traveling together, friends on holiday, couples looking for a restful stay, and small corporate groups who need a practical place to gather in the Nilgiris.

The homestay’s setting near Coonoor Road gives guests a balance of access and calm, so it works well for people who want to see Ooty without staying in the middle of the busiest areas.

  • ✓Personalized service and a family-run feel.
  • ✓Dining and kitchen facilities for flexible stays.
  • ✓Clean, comfortable, and easy to navigate on a phone.

Gemini Homestay is designed for guests who want the comforts of a private home while exploring Ooty. With welcoming spaces, kitchen and dining facilities, and attentive service, it is an easy place to settle into for a holiday in the Nilgiris.
